Graphics

Internal resolutionRenders the game's 640 × 528 image at 1x–4x in both directions. Pick the step whose height is at or above your window height: 2x for a 720p window, 3x for 1080p, 4x for 1440p. Higher steps supersample and cost GPU time; 4x is the maximum, so a 4K window runs slightly below native.

About

      

        
Crash Launcher 

        
Prepares your own copy of Crash of the Titans (PAL or USA) and starts the native build. It contains no game code or assets. The game files are extracted from the disc image you provide, on your PC.
